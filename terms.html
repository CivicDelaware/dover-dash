What Dover Dash is

Dover Dash is an independent, nonpartisan project of Civic Delaware that tracks legislation from the Delaware General Assembly and presents plain-English summaries organized by persona. It's provided as a free public resource.

Content on Dover Dash, including bill summaries and classifications, is interpretive and provided for general informational purposes only. It is not legal, tax, or financial advice. For guidance on how a specific law applies to your situation, consult a licensed attorney or the appropriate professional.

AI-generated content

Bill summaries on Dover Dash are AI-written, in plain English. The official bill synopsis is always shown alongside the AI summary so you can verify it against the primary source yourself. We make reasonable efforts to ensure accuracy but cannot guarantee that every summary is error-free.

Accuracy and primary sources

We source bill data from the Delaware General Assembly's official website (legis.delaware.gov). We work to keep everything current and accurate, but Dover Dash is not an official government resource. If anything on Dover Dash ever disagrees with what's posted on the Delaware General Assembly's own site, the official government version is the correct one.
