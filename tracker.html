Hi Friends, we're back!

I hope everyone has been having a good summer, whether at the "beach," the "shore," or by a "pool" :-)

We took about a month off. Honestly, after the session ended with an all-nighter, we needed it. While we were away, we made some real improvements to Dover Dash, and now we're ready to go again, right on time too, with primaries coming up.

Here's what we built:

What's new

Legislator photos. Look up your rep or senator and you'll now see their photo, plus a direct link to their official bio.

Bills organized by category. Results are now grouped so it's easier to browse and find what actually matters to you.

Remember, we give bills nicknames. Every bill gets a short, plain-English name instead of a legal title, so it's easy to remember and talk about.

See both sides. Every AI summary is shown right next to the bill's official text, so you never have to just take our word for it.

If you want a refresher, head over to the site and check your profile. It only takes a minute.
