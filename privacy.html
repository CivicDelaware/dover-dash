Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

What we collect

When you subscribe for updates, we collect the information you provide: first name, last name, and email address. We don't require an account to use Dover Dash, and browsing the bill tracker itself doesn't require you to submit any personal information.

How we use it

We use your information solely to send you updates about Dover Dash, such as new profiles, new features, or session updates. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Where it's stored

Subscriber information is stored and managed through Mailchimp, our email service provider. Mailchimp has its own privacy practices, available at mailchimp.com/legal/privacy.

Third-party links

Dover Dash links to external sites, primarily the Delaware General Assembly's official site (legis.delaware.gov) and the Delaware Code (delcode.delaware.gov). We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in every email we send. To request that we delete your information entirely, contact us at info@civicdelaware.com.

Children's privacy

Dover Dash is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@civicdelaware.com.
